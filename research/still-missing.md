# Plotline — what's still missing vs. the reference game (2026-10-14)

Built so far: ~1,100 events in 21 packs; 4 visible stats + karma/fame/stress/co-worker & school popularity/craziness/followers; 51 activities;
16 crimes; 88 challenges & achievements; 32 ribbons; 51 job tracks (+ cartel, fighter, fame tracks); job stress/hours/HR/co-workers;
education + welfare-state tuition; ailments/depression/doctor/ER/psychiatrist; assets with mortgages, repairs, scrapping, landlord rent,
accessories, heirlooms; loans/investments/finances; licenses, emigration, fertility, will, lawsuits, bets, sweepstakes, gigs, recruiter,
headhunter offers; engagement → wedding planner, prenup card, best friends/enemies, spying, hookups w/ STD & pregnancy & cheating; mafia,
business, royalty, astronaut, agent; social media with 6 platforms; legacy with heirlooms and will; 5-year time machine; custom people/city;
Surrender; EN + HE.

## Not yet built (ordered by player impact)
1. **Prison depth** — lawyer choice / plea bargain / trial odds; prison tab with prison jobs, gang membership, riots, appeals; the escape maze
   mini-game; probation; death row. (We have: sentences, prison events, parole/escape via events, fugitive recapture.)
2. **Crime mini-games** — burglary house pick, GTA car pick, hitman target pick, hacking target, accomplices. (We have: one-tap crimes with odds.)
3. **Doctor choice** — two doctors with reputation bars; alternative/witch doctor; blood/plasma donation; concern bar on symptom cards.
4. **School extras** — teacher list & interactions, class popularity screen, private-school switch, GED, per-life random major subset,
   explicit scholarship/loan/parents-pay funding card. (We have: grades, study, clubs via events, uni/grad, scholarship by smarts.)
5. **Military** — branches, ranks, deployments, the minesweeper mini-game.
6. **Relationships** — in-laws & grandparents as people, dating apps as a separate flow, paternity/surrogacy/abortion cards, alimony &
   child support after divorce, custody.
7. **Assets** — mortgage *approval* vs. cash, renovation/flipping, boats/planes with condition, collectibles menu, insurance, museum/zoo/casino ownership.
8. **Ribbons** — 32 of 40; missing ones from the reference: Hero, Stupid, Wicked, Mooch, Rowdy, Thief, Highroller, Cunning, Cat Lady,
   Addict, Tarzan, Gold Digger, Movie Buff, Generous, Veteran, Influencer, Academic, Lustful, Scandalous.
9. **Fame depth** — verified badges, sponsorship deals per platform, paparazzi/scandal chains, award shows as a system.
10. **Country mechanics** — per-country tax tables, conscription, legal quirks (we have: monarchy flag, welfare-state free uni/healthcare).
11. **Real-date "Current" events / holidays**, timed community challenges, leaderboards, cemetery of past lives.
12. **Reaction bars on outcome cards** ("Her concern 95%") and a "Surprise me" random-choice button on event cards.
13. **Tutorial pop-overs** in the first year.

## Balance notes still open
- Random-choice lives die around 60; player-driven lives longer. Reference parents died at 76–79.
- Our household economics are tuned to the single Danish life observed; US-style tuition/healthcare lives will be harsher.
