# BitLife (Candywriter) – Genre Design Reference

Purpose: understand the text-based life-sim genre as BitLife defines it, so an **original** game can be built. Compiled only from public sources (store listings, fan wikis/guides, reviews). No app binary or assets were inspected. Fan guides are secondhand and often version-specific; numbers below are indicative, not authoritative. We do not reuse BitLife's text, names or art.

---

## 1. Core loop

- **One tap = one year.** A large "Age" button (bottom centre) advances the character one year. Every press generates: an age header line, a few deadpan one-line life notes, and zero or more **random events**. The game has no natural end-point except death; rounds take minutes, lives take ~10-20 minutes.
- **Random events** appear as modal pop-ups: a short, often absurd paragraph, then 2-4 **multiple-choice responses** (e.g. ignore / confront / apologise / fight / call police). Outcomes modify stats, money, relationships, karma, or create follow-on consequences (lawsuit, arrest, new friend, injury). Event pools are gated by age, country, job, fame, prison status, etc. Guides note that memorising "right" answers does not fully work because outcomes are randomised.
- **Main screen layout (portrait phone):**
  - **Top bar**: character name, age, job title/status, money (bank balance; net worth elsewhere). Tapping the name opens the profile (country, birthday, parents, karma, etc.).
  - **Centre**: scrolling text log of the current life (the "diary"), newest year at the bottom.
  - **Four stat bars** near the bottom, each colour-coded by value (green high -> red low): **Happiness, Health, Smarts, Looks** (0-100%). Hidden stats exist too: karma, fame, athleticism, discipline, craziness, willpower, religiousness, and skills (music, acting, etc.) shown when relevant.
  - **Bottom nav**: five flat icon tabs: **Occupation (job)** | **Assets** | **[Age button]** | **Relationships** | **Activities**. School replaces Occupation while enrolled; Prison replaces it while incarcerated. Royalty/Fame/Business etc. add their own sub-menus.
- **Activities** is the catch-all menu: Mind & Body (gym, library, meditation, plastic surgery), Doctor, Love (date, hookup, dating app), Crime, Casino/Lottery, Movies, Vacation, Emigrate, Military, Adoption, Pets, Black Market, Shopping, Will, Surrender/End Life, and so on.
- **Karma** is tracked silently and rewards kind choices / penalises cruel or criminal ones; it influences luck in events and the eulogy.

## 2. Life stages

1. **Birth**: random country, city, gender, name, parents (with their own jobs/wealth), siblings (sometimes none), starting stats. Rare rolls: royal family, celebrity parents, twins. A "custom life" lets you pick name/gender/country; God Mode (paid) lets you edit everything.
2. **Childhood (0-5)**: little agency; events about parents, pets, siblings; pre-school.
3. **School (~4/6-18)**: automatic enrolment in primary -> middle -> high school. A **grade bar** fluctuates; "Study harder" raises it; skipping class, misbehaving and fights lower it. Options: study harder, go to library (12+), join clubs/sports (basketball, soccer, dance, journalism...; can become captain/president), cliques (cosmetic), part-time jobs (14-16+), freelance gigs (13+), driver's licence test (16, a tiny road-sign quiz), drop out (needs orphan status or age ~15+, parents overrule). Repeated offences -> expulsion; fights can escalate to manslaughter and juvenile detention.
4. **University (18+)**: apply with a chosen **major** (biology, chemistry, maths, finance, English, psychology, computer science, nursing, etc.). Admission depends mainly on Smarts and grades. Pay with **scholarship** (high smarts, ~90+ almost guaranteed), **student loan**, or parents. Community college is the cheap alternative. 3-4 years with their own study/party/cheat events.
5. **Graduate/professional schools**: graduate school, **law** (English/political science/history...), **medical** (biology/chemistry/nursing...), **dental**, **pharmacy**, **veterinary**, **nursing**, **business** (maths/finance). Each is the gate to a career ladder (lawyer -> judge; junior doctor -> surgeon; etc.).
6. **Careers (18-65ish)**: job listings, interviews, promotions, raises, firing, retirement with pension (see section 3).
7. **Old age & death**: health decays, random illnesses, retirement income; death from age, disease, accident, murder, execution, or voluntary "End Life". Death screen = **tombstone** with name, dates, eulogy summary, net worth, **ribbon** (section 7), and stats.
8. **Legacy / generations**: after death the player may **continue as a child** (or other heir), inheriting part of the estate plus assets (an heir must be 18+ to receive a house/car; a **Will** menu lets you split the estate among relatives; an estate tax applies in some countries). Cemetery lets you revisit past lives. Family trees persist across generations.

## 3. Careers & money

- **Job families** (listed under Occupation > Jobs, refreshing yearly): full-time (salaried, promotions, pension), part-time (teens/students; low pay), freelance gigs (one-off, negotiable; ~$9-$1000), military (enlist, deployments, minesweeper minigame, ranks), special careers, criminal "jobs".
- **Listings** show title, company, salary, requirements (degree, licence, skill). **Applying** triggers a short **interview**: one random multiple-choice question; wrong/odd answers lose the job. Hiring also checks criminal record and stats.
- **Promotion** depends on a hidden **performance** meter: "Work harder", hours above expectation, good relationship with supervisor and coworkers, health. **Firing** for low performance, low health, refusing overtime, quarrels with the boss, or scandals; you can also quit, ask for a raise, or get a corporate job and climb a ladder to CEO.
- **Ladders & indicative salaries** (USD, vary by country): janitor/potty cleaner/water-slide tester ~$10-30k; corporate junior -> senior -> VP/CEO $40k-$300k+; police cadet -> chief; junior psychiatrist ~$75k -> senior; lawyer ~$80k -> partner/judge $150k+; pilot (needs flight school/licence) ~$100k+; surgeon/brain surgeon $200k-$500k+. Fame careers (actor, musician, model, athlete) scale with fame to millions. Income is taxed; take-home is shown slightly below the listed salary.
- **Special careers** tab (18+; several require a Job Pack): Actor (drama lessons, auditions, agent, fame), Musician (instrument/voice lessons, band, record deal, solo), Model, Pro Athlete (school teams -> draft -> contracts, MVPs, injuries), Politician (school board -> mayor -> governor -> president; approval and elections), Business (found company, 25 industries, products, employees, competition), Mafia (join family, ranks to Godfather), Street Hustler (busker, panhandler, scammer), Astronaut (STEM degree + pilot licence + space academy; moon/Mars missions), Royalty (born or married in).
- **Assets** tab: real estate (trailer to mansion, mortgage or cash, condition bar, renovate, rent out with Landlord pack, gift, sell), cars/boats/planes (licence needed, depreciate, crash/repair, insurance), jewelry, instruments, pets (shelter, store, breeders, exotic dealer; ailments, vet, tricks, deaths), collectibles from the Black Market. Net worth = cash + asset values - debts.
- **Lottery & casino**: buy tickets yearly (tiny odds, jackpot shown), casino blackjack, horse racing; losses can zero you out. High-roller ribbon for $1M+ lifetime winnings.
- **Investing** (Investor pack): stocks, crypto, bonds, real-estate funds with volatility; base game has none besides property flipping.
- **Taxes**: income tax by country, estate tax on inheritance (some countries have none, which players exploit by emigrating), child support and alimony after divorce.

## 4. Relationships

- **Types**: parents, step-parents, grandparents, siblings/half-siblings, children, pets, friends, best friend, classmates, coworkers/supervisor, lovers (partner, fiance, spouse, ex), hookups, affairs. Each has a **relationship bar** (0-100), age, job, money, looks/smarts/craziness stats, and a mood emoji.
- **Interactions** (per person, once a year each): spend time, conversation, compliment, gift (cost scales), insult, argue, assault, ask for money, movie, hang out, apologise, break up, propose, see the doctor with them, attend funeral.
- **Dating**: "Love" in Activities -> date (random suitor with stats), hookup (STD/pregnancy risk, protection option), dating app (filter by age/looks). Partners can propose, cheat, leave, or ask for prenups.
- **Marriage**: propose with a ring (cost affects acceptance), **prenup** appears when either side is wealthy and protects pre-marriage assets; wedding costs; combined finances; spouse's income contributes.
- **Divorce**: initiated by either side; without a prenup shared assets split roughly 50/50 and alimony/child support may follow. Affairs discovered via pop-ups raise divorce chance.
- **Children**: birth, adoption (needs a home and money, ~$10k), IVF, surrogacy; children have stats and later careers; kids can die, move out, get arrested; grandchildren count for the Fertile ribbon. Parents may pay tuition or give money when asked.
- **Inheritance**: death of parents/spouse yields cash and sometimes a house/car; Will menu allows allocating estate; heirs under 18 don't get property. Continue-as-child is the legacy loop.

## 5. Crime & prison

- **Crimes** (Activities > Crime; age-gated 11/14/16/18): shoplift, porch piracy, pickpocket, burglary (a stealth minigame: grab items, avoid homeowner/dog), grand theft auto (choose vehicle and method), bank robbery (choose bank, weapon, disguise, getaway vehicle; success "very slim"; 11+ years if caught), train robbery (must be at a specific time; secret Bandit ribbon), embezzlement (from employer), murder (choose target and method; drive-by, poison, etc.), hire a hitman, arson, drug dealing, extortion (mafia), plus smuggling events. Success is a probability affected by smarts, athleticism, notoriety, location and luck; guides never found published odds.
- **Arrest & court**: pop-up with charge, then a **lawyer choice** (public defender vs. paid lawyers with skill and cost) and a **plea** (guilty / not guilty); outcome is a sentence in years (or acquittal). Sentences stack; repeat offences lengthen. Lawsuits (sue or be sued) use the same courtroom flow.
- **Prison**: separate tab replaces Occupation. Options: prison job, workout, library, gang (join, respect), appeal, parole hearings, bribes, riots, **escape**. Behaviour affects parole. Juvenile detention for minors.
- **Escape minigame**: grid maze; you move one square per turn, the guard moves two and always tries horizontal moves first; trap the guard against a wall to run free. A fixed set of layouts gets harder each attempt; failure adds years; success leaves you a fugitive (recapture risk, extra years, border arrests). Riots are a snake-style minigame (gather 10 inmates) with small escape chance.
- **Notoriety** rises with crimes, making arrests more likely and gating mafia recruitment; informant minigame if caught in the mafia.

## 6. Premium content

Base game is free with ads (one-time "Remove Ads" ~$3). Prices vary by region and year; US listing (2026) shows Bitizenship $7.99-$9.99, God Mode $9.99, Boss Mode $20.99 (all current and future job packs), Time Machine $0.99 per use, expansion packs ~$5-8 each, individual job packs ~$2-5.

| Pack | One-line mechanic |
|---|---|
| **Bitizenship** | Ad-free, dark mode, extra event choices, premium shops (breeders, exotic pets, plastic surgery options), Fitness, more hair/looks edits, teacher/boss interactions. |
| **God Mode** | Edit nearly any attribute (stats, country, social status, royal status) of your character and any NPC, repeatedly. |
| **Time Machine** | Pay-per-use "undo": rewind to an earlier age (e.g. to avoid a death or bad choice). |
| **Boss Mode** | Bundle of all Job Packs, current and future. |
| **Royalty** (base feature, God-Mode selectable) | Born/married into monarchy in ~17 countries; titles, respect meter, vote on laws, execute subjects, public disservice menu, risk of revolt. |
| **Fame** (base) | Fame bar for public-eye jobs; commercials, photoshoots, talk shows, books, social media followers, awards, scandals, paparazzi. |
| **Mafia / Organized Crime** (job pack) | Join one of six families, complete assigned crimes, pay tribute, gain respect, rise to Godfather, informant minigame. |
| **Business** (job pack) | Found a company in 25 industries; manage facilities, products, inventory, payroll, contracts and competition; buy/sell businesses. |
| **Pro Athlete** (job pack) | School sports -> draft -> club contracts worldwide, MVP awards, injuries and recovery. |
| **Astronaut** (job pack) | STEM degree + pilot licence + Space Academy; ranks cadet -> general unlock lunar and Mars missions; alien encounters. |
| **Secret Agent** (expansion) | Run a spy agency: recruit agents, buy gadgets, hack/seduce/assassinate missions. |
| **Black Market** (expansion; the "dark web" of BitLife) | Six shady dealers (arms, art thief, jewel fencer, street chemist, wildlife smuggler, antiques), fake-item risk, appraisals, museum and storage-unit auctions. |
| **Landlord** (expansion) | Buy rental properties, upgrade via contractors, manage tenants and rents. |
| **Investor** (expansion) | Stocks, crypto, bonds and funds with volatility. |
| **Cult** (expansion) | Found a commune, recruit followers, rituals, run from authorities. |
| **Teacher** (base career since ~2023) | Teach, grade, handle students; school-based events. |
| **Others** | Model, Politician, Musician, Actor, Street Hustler, Zoo, Outdoor Lifestyle, Racing, Challenge Vault (replay weekly challenges), Assassin's Blade (guaranteed murder success). |

## 7. Ribbons (awarded at death)

Exactly **one ribbon per life**, chosen by a priority order and shown on the tombstone; the Ribbons screen collects them. About 40 exist. Representative set with triggers:

Academic (post-graduate degree), Addict (die with 2+ addictions), Barbie Girl (many plastic surgeries), Cat Lady (many cats), Cunning (escape prison and stay free), Deadly (5+ kills), Family Guy (husband with wife, 2+ kids, high family bars), Famous (achieve fame), Fertile (many kids/grandkids), Generous (many gifts), Geriatric (live to ~120), Globetrotter (visit 20+ countries), Gold Digger (marry rich, never work), Hero (save a life), High Roller ($1M casino winnings), Houdini (10 escapes), Influencer (1M+ followers), Jailbird (long prison time), Lazy (do nothing), Loaded ($20M+ net worth), Lustful (20+ hookups), Mediocre (average, nothing notable), Monopoly (10+ houses bought/sold), Mooch (beg for money 10+ times), Movie Buff (20+ movies), Rich ($2.5M-$20M), Rowdy (clubbing, parties), Scandalous (several years jailed), Stupid (low smarts), Successful ($250k-$2.5M, house, car, family), Tarzan (20 exotic pets), Thief (many thefts, never caught), Unlucky (die young from disease/accident), Veteran (military career), Wasteful (End Life early), Wicked (STDs/cheating). Secret: Bandit (rob a train), Big Boss (company president, $20M), Model Bitizen (perfect karma), Teammate (work at the dev studio).

Separate **achievements** (~150+) reward milestones (first job, win lottery, become president...). Weekly **Challenges** (4-5 objectives, themed) drive retention and often require packs.

## 8. UI & tone notes

- **Text-first**: no character art beyond a round avatar/emoji; the life is a scrolling diary. Pop-ups are plain cards with a title, body text, and stacked option buttons.
- **Emoji everywhere**: each menu item, event, asset and person has an emoji icon; stat bars are labelled with emoji. Lists are tall flat rows with emoji + label + right-aligned value.
- **Humour**: deadpan, sardonic, slightly dark; realistic tragedies (cancer, divorce, overdoses) sit beside absurd events (grandma joins a biker gang, a bear in the backyard). Second-person voice ("You are..."), short sentences, punchline last. Mature content (sex, drugs, violence) under a 17+/PEGI 16 rating.
- **Colour scheme (general)**: a muted dark navy/teal header and nav, cream/white content panes, and saturated flat accent colours for tabs (greens, blues, oranges); stat bars shift green -> yellow -> red. Dark mode is a paid perk. Buttons are large rounded rectangles, high contrast, thumb-friendly. Confirmation dialogs use iOS-native alert styling.
- **Bottom nav** with a prominent central Age button is the signature control; secondary menus are full-screen lists with a back arrow; profile is reached by tapping the name.

## 9. What players complain about (opportunities)

1. **Monetisation creep**: features that were free became paid; many weekly challenges need packs; dark mode behind a paywall; two Bitizenship SKUs confuse buyers.
2. **Ads**: 30-40 s ads on routine actions (lessons, gifts), sometimes even after buying ad removal.
3. **Lost purchases** when switching phones; "Restore purchases" failing; slow support.
4. **Repetition**: the same random events recur across lives; event pool feels thin after a few hours; mid-life (30-60) is a grind of tapping Age.
5. **Shallow relationships**: siblings and extended family (grandparents, cousins) barely interact; spouses and kids have few meaningful arcs; family tab omits relatives.
6. **Opaque RNG**: interview questions unrelated to the job; record deals/awards feel random even with maxed skills; crime odds unknown; grinding by save-reload is the common workaround.
7. **Delayed/overhyped updates**, bugs (triplets labelled twins), and lack of multiplayer or shared worlds.
8. **Parent concern**: mature content with no in-app parental controls or age verification.

Design takeaways for an original game: generous free core with transparent odds, a bigger and context-aware event pool with multi-year story arcs, deeper family/friend simulation, visible performance and relationship mechanics, cloud-saved entitlements, and optional light-hearted or mature content toggles.
