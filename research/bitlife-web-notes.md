## 2. Stats and hidden stats
- Visible: Happiness, Health, Smarts, Looks (0-100); low = red bar with Boost ad button [pt]. Slow yearly drift (~-1) [pt].
- Other bars: Fame, Wealth (Luxury Lifestyle), Relationship per person, Performance (job), Grades, Popularity (class, co-workers), Stress (Schedule), Reputation (doctors, lawyers), Condition (house, car), skill bars (instrument, acting, martial arts).
- Hidden attributes (editable in God Mode for family) [web]: Karma, Fertility, Craziness, Willpower, Discipline, Sexuality, Happiness.
  - Karma: shapes luck in challenges and longevity; raised by good acts, meditation; lowered by crime [web].
  - Discipline: boosts school/work performance, resists addiction; meditation after 12 raises it [web].
  - Willpower: resisting tempting bad behaviour [web].
  - Fertility: ability to conceive; visible on meditation card [pt]; falls with age/STD.
  - Craziness: shown on friend/date cards [pt]; high = erratic, violent, stalker partners.
- Movers: gym +8 health ($30), library +4 smarts (free), meditate +4 health [pt]; doctor cure +8 health, +16 happiness [pt]; therapy; diet; surgery; illness; bereavement; poverty/unemployment depress happiness and health [pt].
- Death odds climb with age and low health.

## 3. School
- Primary then secondary; length by country (Denmark 9 + 2) [pt]. Public/private; Change Schools (ask to go private); Study Harder; Nurse; Class (popularity bar, classmates); Faculty (teachers: befriend, grade help, flirt, insult); Drop Out [pt].
- Misbehaviour: detention, suspension, expulsion; cheating; skipping class.
- Clubs and teams (secondary): football, basketball, baseball, soccer, track, cheer, drama, debate, band, chess, newspaper, student council, art. Tryout depends on looks/smarts/athleticism; effects: popularity, friends, skill, scholarship chance.
- Popularity: high unlocks prom, dates; low invites bullying.
- Dropping out (from ~16); GED later.
- University: choose a major from a per-life subset (10 shown) [pt]; approx full list: Anthropology, Art History, Biology, Business, Chemistry, Computer Science, Criminal Justice, Dance, Drama, Economics, Education, Engineering, English, Finance, Graphic Design, History, Journalism, Law, Math, Medicine, Music, Nursing, Philosophy, Physics, Political Science, Psychology, Sociology.
- Funding: parents, scholarship (grades/athletics), student loan, or free in some countries [pt]. Part-time campus jobs. Schedule/Stress bar. No full-time jobs while enrolled [pt].
- Grad schools: Law (3y), Medical (4y + residency), Dental, MBA, Masters, PhD, Vet, Pharmacy, Nursing; need related bachelor + good grades.
- "Go back to school" row in Occupation menu [pt].

## 4. Jobs
- Full-time listings refresh yearly: ~37 at 18, sorted by salary; degree jobs shown but rejected without degree [pt]. Ranges: no-degree $14-50k; degree $63-93k; professional $170-205k [pt].
- Part-time hourly, ~9+ h/week, no interview [pt]. Freelance gigs: babysitter/dog walker/pet sitter $13/h up to handyman $32/h, choose your rate (higher rate = lower hire chance) [pt]. Job Recruiter: $1,600 fee, guaranteed offer with interview [pt].
- Interview: one question, one sensible answer of four [pt].
- Job menu: Performance bar, Co-Workers (popularity; befriend, flirt, hook up, prank, insult, report), Hours, Human Resources, Resign, Retire, Work Harder, raise/promotion request [pt].
- Auto raises ~10% [pt]; promotion after years + performance (+46%) [pt]; random firing with reason text [pt].
- Retirement from ~62; pension/social security per country.
- Military: Army, Navy, Air Force, Marines, Coast Guard; 9 promotions (Veteran ribbon) [web]; deployments (injury/death/PTSD), minesweeper-style combat minigame; conscription in some countries.
- Special careers (pack-gated originally): Actor, Astronaut, Business (to CEO; Big Boss ribbon [web]), Dealer/cartel, Mafia, Model, Music Producer, Musician, Politician, Pro Athlete, Street Hustler, Fighter, Movie Director, Secret Agent [pt].
- 164 collectible careers [pt].
- Stress: hours and difficulty drive Stress, which hurts health/happiness.

## 6. Health
- Ailments: ear infection, cold, flu, bronchitis, asthma, migraine, hypertension, diabetes, many cancers, heart disease, stroke, dementia, kidney/liver failure, STDs, depression, anxiety, eating disorders, fractures, hearing/vision loss.
- Doctors: choose 1 of 2 with Reputation bars; treatment can fail [pt]; free in socialized countries [pt]. Alternative doctor, witch doctor (expansion), ER, optometrist, psychiatrist, blood/plasma donation [pt].
- Plastic surgery: many procedures; can botch; Barbie Girl needs 30 [web].
- Addictions: alcohol, drugs, gambling, smoking, sex; rehab; Addict ribbon needs 2 active at death [web].
- Lifespan roughly 70-90 with early random deaths; Geriatric = 100+ [web]. Causes: cancer, heart, stroke, accident, suicide, murder, execution, overdose, war, old age.

## 8. Crime and prison
- Menu categories (12): bank robbery, burglary, embezzle, grand theft auto, hack, hitman, mischief, murder, pickpocket, porch pirate, shoplift, train robbery [pt]; accomplice pick [pt]; kids limited to mischief/pickpocket/porch pirate/shoplift [pt].
- Arrest -> lawyer (public/private/none) -> plea -> trial -> sentence (months to decades; death penalty in some places); self-defence/diminished capacity mitigate [web, weak].
- Prison menu: work, exercise, library, fight, join gang, riot (optional), appeal, solitary, escape. Escape maze: grid, guard moves 2 per your 1, trap him to pass, once a year, harder each try, failure adds years [web]. Post-escape fugitive: border arrest, extra years [web]. Parole on good behaviour and probation after release (unconfirmed [web]). Death row for capital crimes.
- Juvenile detention for minors.
